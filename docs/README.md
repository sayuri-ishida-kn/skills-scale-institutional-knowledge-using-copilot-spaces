# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

Welcome to OctoAcme's central entry point for project management process documentation. These guides describe how cross-functional teams plan, execute, and deliver product features, services, and integrations.

## Approach and Core Principles

OctoAcme follows a structured, iterative lifecycle: Initiation, Planning, Execution, Release, and Close & Retrospective. Risk management and communication support every phase, and lessons learned feed into future work.

- **Customer-first:** Prioritize customer value and usability.
- **Iterative delivery:** Deliver small, testable increments.
- **Clear ownership:** Each project has a named Project Manager (PM) and Product Lead.
- **Data-informed decisions:** Define success metrics, measure impact, and iterate based on evidence.
- **Psychological safety:** Encourage feedback, candor, and learning.

New team members should start with the reference guides below, then follow the lifecycle guides for their current project phase. Each guide contains the relevant activities, templates, or checklists.

## Project Lifecycle Guides

1. [Project Initiation](./octoacme-project-initiation.md) — Validate the business need, define measurable outcomes, align stakeholders, and decide whether to proceed to planning. Prepare a Project One-pager, stakeholder list, high-level timeline, and initial risks.
2. [Project Planning](./octoacme-project-planning.md) — Turn an approved initiative into a prioritized, estimated backlog with acceptance criteria and a Definition of Done. Identify dependencies, respect team capacity, and agree on milestones and a release plan.
3. [Execution & Tracking](./octoacme-execution-and-tracking.md) — Coordinate delivery through project boards, standups, delivery syncs, and demos. Use small pull requests, reviews, testing, and security scanning to maintain quality; track success metrics and escalate blockers.
4. [Release & Deployment](./octoacme-release-and-deployment.md) — Confirm acceptance criteria, passing CI and security scans, release notes, and a rollback plan. Verify deployments in staging and production, announce releases, and use incident response or rollback when needed.
5. [Retrospective & Continuous Improvement](./octoacme-retrospective-and-continuous-improvement.md) — Reflect after sprints, releases, milestones, and incidents. Prioritize improvements, track action items with owners and due dates, follow up, and measure their impact.

## Throughout the Lifecycle

- [Risk Management & Communication](./octoacme-risks-and-communication.md) — Identify, assess, mitigate, and monitor risks in a risk register with owners and mitigation plans. Keep stakeholders informed through regular status updates, communicate incidents, and escalate through the team, PM, Product Lead, and Sponsor as appropriate.
